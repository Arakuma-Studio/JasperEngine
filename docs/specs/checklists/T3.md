# T3 manual check (AC46, and what this host cannot draw)

Nothing here runs on the build host: no GPU, no phone, no Windows, macOS or iOS. Record each result in the report that runs it.

## Campaign GPU (AC46)

On the RTX machine of `docs/perf/gpu-campaign.md`, at 4K, `python3 tools/perf/terrain_tiers.py --editor <editor> --resolution 3840x2160`:

| Fixture | Medium against High | Low against High |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain Baseline | at most 0.9x | at most 0.6x |
| Terrain Nine | at most 0.5x | at most 0.3x |

## Android, mid-range 2022 (AC22, AC46)

1. On a host with `/dev/kvm`: `ANDROID_PROJECT=terrain-materials ANDROID_ABIS=x86_64 tools/build.sh android`, then `tools/android-emulator-test.sh`:
   the centre of the screen is the sky through a hole, with no GLES error in logcat.
2. On the phone at 1080p, Low: the terrain pass at most 4 ms in the Profiler's GPU Usage.

## D3D11 (Windows)

Open a project with a holed, painted terrain at each of High, Medium and Low: the hole's edge is texel sharp, the shadow has the hole, and there
is no shader error in the console. Macro Noise, Distance Resampling and 12 layers draw as on Linux.

## Metal and iOS

`cmake -S engine -B build-engine -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release -DJASPERENGINE_TESTS=ON && cmake --build build-engine --target jasperengine-core-tests && build-engine/tests/core/jasperengine-core-tests --gtest_filter=TerrainMaterialsGpu*:TerrainHolesGpu*:TerrainShadowsGpu*`.
iOS: `python3 tools/xcode_sync_engine.py --check`, then `terrain-materials` from the Xcode workspace.
