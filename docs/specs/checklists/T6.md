# T6 manual check (R81, AC40-AC44): what this host cannot run

Run on real hardware. Each step names what to see; report the device and the numbers.

## Desktop GPU (Windows, macOS)

1. Open a project with a Voxel Volume of 256x128x256 made from the Cave Entrance preset over a Terrain.
2. Fly the camera through the cave mouth at Quality High. The Voxel Volume panel's stats stay under 300
   chunks and 600 k triangles; no crack or flicker shows at a LOD seam or at the hole's edge.
3. Note the frame time with the volume on and off; a disabled volume costs nothing (R58).

## Android and iOS (Quality Auto: Low)

1. Build `tools/test-projects/voxels` for the device and run it: the log shows the `[gate] voxels` lines of
   `tools/test-projects/voxels/android-expect.txt`, the hash included.
2. Dig repeatedly for 5 minutes with a Lua loop. The frame rate stays steady, the device does not throttle,
   and the panel's memory stays within 64 MiB.
3. Under 120 chunks and 150 k triangles from any camera (R57).

## Determinism (arm64, Windows, macOS)

1. `jasperengine-core-tests --gtest_filter=VoxelDeterminism.*` passes with the recorded hash.
