# T5 manual check (AC40)

Run in an isolated editor on a scratch project, with a Terrain of a few hundred units, a grass model
and two tree models. Each step names what to see. Screenshots go with the record of the run, not into
the repository. A software renderer proves shape, not feel: the last section needs real devices.

## Paint Trees

1. Open the Terrain window, Paint Trees. Drop a model on the grid. Its thumbnail shows, selected.
2. Drag over the ground. Trees appear under the brush at Tree Density, no two nearer than Spacing, with a
   decal that follows the surface. The stroke feels immediate on a 1,000-tree scene.
3. Hold Shift and drag: every prototype goes. Hold Ctrl+Shift: only the selected one goes.
4. Alt+click a tree of another prototype with no drag: it becomes the selected one. Alt+drag orbits.
5. Esc during a stroke: the stroke is gone. Ctrl+Z after one: the step reads "Paint Trees (Island)".
6. Mass Place Trees: Preview Count gives a number; Place gives that number; Cancel stops; the scene stays
   usable while it runs; undo reads "Mass Place Trees (Island)".

## Paint Details

7. Paint Details: the rows read Min/Max Width, Min/Max Height, Healthy Color, Dry Color, Noise Spread,
   Bend Factor, Draw range. Painting grass follows the brush with no hitch at the stroke's end.
8. Show Density: the heat map follows the painted map. Off: nothing left of it.

## Look

9. Walk through the field: grass thins with distance with no popping ring; nothing refills behind you.
10. Wind on: grass and trees sway in waves; their shadows move with them.
11. Trees past Billboard Start: the band dithers from mesh to impostor with no square ring in motion. Bake
    Impostor shows Baked; touching the model file shows Out of Date.
12. Stats at 2 Hz: Estimate For Mobile goes to warning colours on a scene over its budgets.

## Devices

13. D3D11 (Windows): the same scene draws the same, no validation errors, frame time noted.
14. Android and iOS at Low: the benchmark scene's path, frame time and memory noted against R40-R42.
