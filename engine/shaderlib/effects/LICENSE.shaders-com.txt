Effects of the JasperEngine HTML UI ported from shaders.com

These UI effects of engine/shaderlib/effects are ports of shaders.com effects
(https://shaders.com/, https://github.com/shader-effects-inc/shaders, packages/core/src/shaders/<Name>)
to GLSL, under the MIT license below:

  aurora                shaders.com Aurora
  block-dissolve        shaders.com BlockDissolve
  color-wheel           shaders.com ColorWheel
  conic-spin            shaders.com ConicGradient (adapted: speed in turns/s)
  curl-noise            shaders.com CurlNoise
  cursor-ripples        shaders.com CursorRipples (adapted: analytic ripple events replace the wave grid, CursorRipples/index.ts:12-26)
  cursor-trail          shaders.com CursorTrail (adapted: CPU trail buffer replaces the fluid grid)
  diamond-gradient      shaders.com DiamondGradient
  diamond-wipe          shaders.com DiamondWipe
  dot-grid              shaders.com DotGrid
  emboss                shaders.com Emboss (adapted: relief from the content alpha)
  falling-lines         shaders.com FallingLines
  flowing-gradient      shaders.com FlowingGradient
  fractal-noise         shaders.com FractalNoise
  glow                  shaders.com Glow
  godrays               shaders.com Godrays
  gradient-pan          shaders.com LinearGradient (adapted: speed scrolls the stops)
  grid                  shaders.com Grid
  hex-grid              shaders.com HexGrid
  hologram              shaders.com Hologram (adapted: applied to the element's content instead of a shape)
  holographic           shaders.com Holographic (adapted: shape = element box; tilt follows the pointer)
  iris-wipe             shaders.com IrisWipe
  light-edge            shaders.com LightEdge (adapted: shape = element rounded box, sizes in px)
  light-leak            shaders.com LightLeak
  linear-wipe           shaders.com LinearWipe
  marble                shaders.com Marble
  mesh-gradient         shaders.com MeshGradient
  multi-point-gradient  shaders.com MultiPointGradient
  neon-border           shaders.com Neon (adapted: tube along the border, sizes in px)
  noise-dissolve        shaders.com NoiseDissolve (+ original burn edge)
  perlin-noise          shaders.com PerlinNoise
  pixelate              shaders.com Pixelate (adapted: cell size in px)
  plasma                shaders.com Plasma
  radial-pulse          shaders.com RadialGradient (adapted: speed moves rings outward)
  radial-wipe           shaders.com RadialWipe
  ripple-rings          shaders.com Ripples
  ripple-wipe           shaders.com RippleWipe
  simplex-noise         shaders.com SimplexNoise
  sine-wave             shaders.com SineWave
  sparkle               shaders.com Sparkle
  strands               shaders.com Strands
  stripes               shaders.com Stripes
  sunburst              shaders.com SunBurst
  swirl                 shaders.com Swirl (adapted: source `blend` renamed `balance`)
  thin-film             shaders.com ThinFilm (adapted: rim along the element edges)
  venetian-blinds       shaders.com VenetianBlinds
  voronoi               shaders.com Voronoi

These effects are original but follow parts of the shaders.com kit:

  frosted-glass  original (Glass kit glassTint/glassBorderHighlight, gpu/kit/effects/glass.ts:170-199)
  grain          original (FilmGrain look, gpu/kit/colorOps.ts:267)

The include library engine/shaderlib/effects/lib (jfx_color, jfx_hash, jfx_noise, jfx_sdf,
jfx_blend, jfx_tone and the other jfx_*.glsl files) ports functions of the shaders.com kit
(packages/core/src/gpu/kit) under the same license.

Generated by tools/htmlui/gen_effects_docs.py from the effects' @source lines.
