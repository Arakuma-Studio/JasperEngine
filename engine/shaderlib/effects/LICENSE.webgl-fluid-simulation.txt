The JasperEngine fluid simulation ported from Pavel Dobryakov's WebGL-Fluid-Simulation

The fluid simulation of JasperEngine is a port of WebGL-Fluid-Simulation by Pavel Dobryakov
(https://github.com/PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation, script.js) to GLSL and C++, under the
MIT license below:

  engine/shaderlib/effects/lib/fam_fluid_solver.glsl   his solver kernels (curl, vorticity,
                                                       divergence, pressure, gradient subtract,
                                                       advection, splat)
  engine/shaderlib/effects/lib/fam_fluid_look.glsl     his display, bloom, sunrays and colour
                                                       helpers
  engine/shaderlib/fluid.vert, fluid.frag              the native passes on those kernels
  engine/shaderlib/effects/fluid.frag                  the HTML UI effect effect(fluid) on them
  engine/core/fluid/                                   his step(), render(), splats, pointers,
                                                       defaults and the presets built on them
                                                       (FluidSimulator, FluidPresets)

Used by the FluidSimulation component and the HTML UI `fluid` effect. His blue-noise
dithering texture (LDR_LLL1_0.png) is not included: the dither is procedural.
