WebGL-Fluid-Simulation (https://github.com/PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation)

JasperEngine's fluid simulation ports Pavel Dobryakov's WebGL-Fluid-Simulation to GLSL:
the solver and look kernels engine/shaderlib/effects/lib/fam_fluid_solver.glsl and
fam_fluid_look.glsl, the native Fluid Simulation's shader engine/shaderlib/fluid.frag and the
HTML UI effect engine/shaderlib/effects/fluid.frag (effect(fluid)). An export whose shaders use
them ships this notice as licenses/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation-LICENSE.txt. The original is under the
