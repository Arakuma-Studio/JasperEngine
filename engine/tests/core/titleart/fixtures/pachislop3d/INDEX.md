Notes on the files:

- Each `<id>.json` is the StylePreset `p3(...)` returns, without `name`; `group` ("3D") and `builtin: true` are kept as p3 sets them.
  `f-3d-rainbow-glass-gold` also carries the top-level `links` its factory adds after p3 (`{"gold": {"name": "Gold"}}`).
- `uid()` ids (bands, lights, effects) are numbered `id1`, `id2`, ... per look in document order of the JSON
  (style3d bands, then lighting.lights, then effects), not in JavaScript evaluation order.
- Numbers are the doubles JavaScript computes (letter3D depth / offset sums such as `9.600000000000001` are kept as is).
- Effect params are the registry defaults (src/effects/a/glow.ts, finish.ts) with the look's overrides; outerGlow keeps the default
  `gradient` and `angle` even when `fill` is solid, deepGlow keeps `color` with `colorMode` layer, as `fx()` fills every param.
- `lighting` is `fullLighting(...)`: the default comp environment / two default lights / neutral tone mapping / exposure 1,
  overridden by the look's environment, tone mapping, exposure and (when set) lights.
